ECHO VAULT

The vault is pitch black. Send a sonar PING to see the walls.
But guards hear every ping. Collect all shards, then reach the exit.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD  •  Ping: Space  •  Mobile: on-screen buttons

How to play Echo Vault

Echo Vault is a free stealth puzzle game that runs right in your browser – no download, no sign-up. You are a thief inside a vault with the lights off. The walls are invisible until you send out a sonar ping. The ring of sound expands outward and briefly lights up the maze around you.

The catch: guards can hear your pings. Any guard within range will rush to the spot where you pinged. Ping too often and you will be hunted; ping too rarely and you will wander into dead ends. Every level asks you to balance information against noise.

Goal of each level

  • Collect every golden shard hidden in the vault.
  • Once all shards are collected the exit door unlocks (it turns green).
  • Reach the exit without getting caught. You have 3 lives in total.

Controls

  • Move: Arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Ping: Space bar (1.1 second cooldown).
  • Mobile: use the on-screen D-pad and PING button below the game.

Scoring

Each level gives 100 × level points, 50 per shard, a speed bonus for finishing quickly, and a small penalty for every ping you use. Your best score is stored locally in your browser.

Tips and strategy

  • Ping once at the start of each level to get a feel for the layout, then move in short bursts.
  • After you ping, move away from the ping spot – guards walk to where the sound came from.
  • You can always faintly see the tiles right next to you, so use corridors' edges to navigate silently.
  • Guards appear as red glows only when revealed by a ping or when they are very close. Listen for danger by watching the map.
  • Deeper levels have bigger mazes, more guards and faster patrols, so plan your pings carefully.

About the game

Echo Vault is an independent browser game built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every level is generated procedurally with a randomized depth-first maze algorithm plus extra loops, so no two runs are identical. New features and levels are added over time. Read more on the About page.
