About Echo Vault

Echo Vault is a free, independent browser game. The idea is simple: you are in a completely dark vault and the only way to see is to send out a sonar ping – but the guards can hear it too. Every level is a small risk-management puzzle.

Why we made it

Most browser games are either pure reflex or pure puzzle. We wanted a game where information has a price: the more you reveal, the more danger you attract. It plays well in short sessions on both desktop and phone.

How levels are made

Each maze is generated on the fly with a randomized depth-first search algorithm, then extra openings are added so there are multiple routes. Guard count, patrol speed and maze size increase as you progress, so the game keeps getting harder.

Technology

The game is written in plain HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript with no external libraries. It runs entirely in your browser and your best score is stored locally on your device.

Roadmap
