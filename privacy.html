Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Echo Vault ("we", "the site") handles information when you visit and play the game.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts and we do not collect names, emails or other personal details through the game. Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device and is never sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party services

Third-party vendors, including Google, may collect data such as your IP address, browser type and pages viewed in order to deliver and measure ads. We do not control these third parties' cookies.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law, a consent banner will ask for your permission before personalized ads are shown.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest revision.
